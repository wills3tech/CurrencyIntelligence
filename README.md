Currency Intelligence
A calm market workspace for currency reference rates
What it is
Currency Intelligence is a clean, focused tool that helps you see the current value of currencies — especially the Nigerian Naira — with context, without the noise of trading platforms.
It gives you:
Live reference rates
Simple conversion
A focused list of important pairs
The ability to watch pairs and set personal alerts
Everything is designed to feel calm and useful, not overwhelming.
Main features
Dashboard
Your starting point. Quick overview of the workspace, status of data, and a live conversion preview.
Converter
Translate any amount from one currency to another using the same reference rates that power the rest of the app. Clean, clear, and fast.
Markets
Browse important currency pairs.
Special focus on the Naira desk (USD/NGN, GBP/NGN, EUR/NGN, etc.), plus major crosses and other regions.
Watchlist
Save the pairs you care about most. The list stays on your device.
Insights
Short, descriptive market context based on the current reference rates.
Alerts
Set a simple threshold for any pair you are watching. The app checks it when data updates.
Settings
Control how the app looks and behaves (theme, notifications, etc.).
Important notes
This is a reference tool, not a trading platform.
Rates are indicative and come from a data provider (currently ExchangeRate-API).
No accounts, no login required.
Watchlist and alerts are stored locally on your device.
The design is intentionally calm and professional — made for people who just want a clear read on the numbers.
Who it’s for
People who regularly deal with Naira and foreign currencies
Anyone who wants a simple, beautiful place to check rates and set personal alerts
Users who prefer clarity over complexity
